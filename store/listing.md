**Description**（上限 4000）

```
Little fish are trapped in bubbles — and only you can bring them home!

Tap a bubble to free a fish and send it to the tank that wants it. Fill a tank with three of a kind and it swims away happy. But the waiting slots are few, so think before you tap: some bubbles hold a whole queue of fish, and only the one in front can come out.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap fish in the bubbles to send them to matching tanks
• Three of a kind fill a tank
• Fish with no tank to go to wait in the slots — don't let them fill up!
• Plan your order: in queue bubbles only the front fish can be taken

FEATURES
• 360 hand-tuned levels across 18 seas — from Sunrise Shore and Coral Garden to Clockwork Bay and Melody Cove
• 705 original sea creatures to collect in 18 albums, from common friends to dazzling epic ones
• Four helpful skills when you get stuck: Shuffle, Blow, Net and Current
• Combos, a daily challenge, a lucky wheel, daily sign-in rewards and tasks
• Calm, colorful underwater scenes and gentle sounds
• Play offline, anytime

Relax with an easy level, or take on a tricky one that makes you think. How many fish can you rescue?
```
