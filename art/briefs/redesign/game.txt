This is an ORIGINAL UI design mockup for our own casual mobile ocean puzzle game, portrait phone screen 1024x1536 (9:16-ish). Use the attached image ONLY as the reference for how our own sea creatures look (round, big glossy eyes, simple bold shapes, saturated colors); draw creatures in that same look. UI design language (new, must be followed strictly): modern FLAT casual UI. Every button and panel is a solid-color rounded rectangle (corner radius about a quarter of its height) with NO gradient, NO glossy highlight, NO outline, and only ONE thick darker band of the same hue along its bottom edge (about 10 percent of its height) so it looks slightly raised. Text and numerals are clean rounded bold white with NO outline. Generous spacing, strict alignment to a grid, few elements, calm and airy. Color roles: fresh green = main action, bright blue = secondary, amber = reward, soft white translucent = containers. The background is a soft, slightly desaturated teal-to-deep-blue underwater scene with gentle light rays, blurred distant coral and a pale sandy floor, deliberately quieter than the foreground so creatures and UI pop.
